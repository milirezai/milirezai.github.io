What it's for

As an app grows, business rules tend to multiply and tangle — different discounts for VIP users, feature access by permission level, pricing that shifts with conditions. MiliRulePilot centralizes that logic into reusable, composable rule definitions instead of nested conditionals.

Roadmap

  • Fixing a naming typo in the core evaluation method.
  • Adding AND/OR condition grouping, so rules can express compound logic rather than a single flat condition.
  • Expanding the set of comparison operators available when building a condition.
