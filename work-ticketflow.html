ticketFlow

What it does

  • Authentication with role- and permission-based access control (admin, agent, customer).
  • Full ticket management: creation, status transitions, threaded conversations between customer and agent.
  • Notifications on ticket updates, and reporting endpoints for support-team metrics.
  • SLA-based escalation: unanswered tickets past a threshold are automatically flagged and escalated.

My role

Built as a two-person team where both of us worked on the backend. The project was mine to run: I managed branches and pull requests, did code review, defined the issues and tasks, and divided the work between us — taking on the larger share myself.

Escalation logic

A scheduled job periodically checks open tickets against their SLA threshold. Any ticket left unanswered past that window gets flagged and routed to a higher support tier — instead of relying on agents to notice a stale ticket manually.
